🌱 How to Use Your Dog Planters

🖨️ 3D Printing Instructions:

  • Material: PLA+ or PETG (food-safe for herbs)
  • Layer Height: 0.2mm for best detail
  • Infill: 15-20% (keeps it lightweight)
  • Supports: Tree supports for overhangs
  • Post-Processing: Light sanding for smooth finish

🪴 Planting Instructions:

  • Drainage: Add gravel at bottom (holes included)
  • Soil: Use appropriate potting mix for your plants
  • Size Matching: Small planters = small plants
  • Location: Indoor/outdoor based on breed size
  • Watering: Let excess water drain through holes

🎨 Customization Options:

  • Colors: Paint with plant-safe acrylic paints
  • Spots: Add breed-specific markings
  • Scaling: Resize STLs for different plant sizes
  • Personalization: Add dog names or decorations
